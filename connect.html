Connect

Life is meant to be lived in community. Here's how kids, students, and adults actually plug in at Milwaukee Avenue.

Youth

“Rejoice in your youth, and let your heart cheer you in the days of your youth.”

Infants, Toddlers & Preschool

6 months – 4 years

This group meets in engaging, age-appropriate classes. Volunteers teach directly from the Bible, using puppets, games, crafts, and singing, to help kids learn more about God's Word. We foster excitement to grow in knowledge and in love for Christ.

Elementary School

Kindergarten – 5th grade

Broken into three separate classes based on age. Kids read from the Bible, memorize verses, and experience thought-provoking activities, games, fellowship, and prayer as they discover the truths of God and develop lifelong skills.

Middle & High School

6th – 12th grade

Teens today have a vital role to play within the church. Our goal is to help them understand who they are in Christ, and how the gifts, talents, and abilities God has given them can greatly impact the world around them — equipping them for current and future ministry.

College Age

Local college & university students

A small but growing group of college students calls Milwaukee Avenue home. We gather for casual fellowship, meals, and Bible study built around a college schedule — reach out to get plugged into what's currently meeting.

Care Groups

“You are the light of the world… let your light shine before people.”

Matthew 5:14, 16

Life is meant to be lived in community. We grow up in families, socialize and play sports throughout school, and work in teams throughout our careers. Groups shape who we are, and we shape those around us. It's through our relationships that we experience the world and deal with the rewards and issues that befall us.

It's natural to form groups at church. We love socializing and worshiping with those who share experiences and lives such as our own. We find so many things in common that we relate immediately with people who may share our profession, children, or any hobby and interest that we may have.

Care Groups are informal gatherings to get to know each other better while serving the church and the Lubbock community. They include members of our congregation and our frequent visitors. It's a fun time to gather with our brothers and sisters in Christ for social activities, prayers, devotionals, and service.

Want to join a Care Group?

Reach out and we'll connect you with a group near you, or one that shares your season of life.
