Privacy Policy

What information this site collects, why, and how it's handled.

This page is a draft template for review — it describes this design preview as built, but should be read and adjusted by the church's own leadership (and legal counsel, if desired) before it's published on the live site.

Milwaukee Avenue Church of Christ ("we," "us," "our") operates this website to serve our congregation and community. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us directly, primarily through our Request Info form: your name, email address, and optionally your phone number and a message, along with the topic you select. If you give online, our giving partner (Sharefaith) collects the payment and contact information needed to process your gift — we don't see or store your card details ourselves. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers on this site, and we don't sell or rent any information you give us.

How we use it

We use the information you submit only to respond to your request — for example, to follow up about visiting, a Bible study, an event, or a question for one of our elders or ministers. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your information with outside organizations without your permission, except with service providers who help us operate the site (like our giving platform) and only as needed to provide that service.

Third-party services embedded on this site

Some pages embed content from outside services, each governed by its own privacy policy: YouTube (sermon and podcast video), Buzzsprout, Apple Podcasts, and Spotify (podcast audio), Google Maps (directions), Google Fonts (page typography), and Sharefaith (online giving). These services may collect information such as your IP address or device type when their embedded content loads, independent of anything we collect ourselves.

Children's information

Some of our programs, like Vacation Bible School, are for children, and a parent or guardian may submit information on a child's behalf through our forms. We only use that information to communicate with the parent or guardian about the program and don't knowingly collect information directly from children.

Cookies

This site itself does not set tracking or advertising cookies. Embedded third-party content (such as YouTube or Buzzsprout players) may set its own cookies when you interact with it, subject to that provider's policy.

Data retention & security

We keep information you submit only as long as needed to respond to your request and for our normal church records, and we take reasonable steps to protect it. No method of storing or transmitting information online is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we have on file for you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by reaching out through our Request Info form or by calling the church office.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update this page and revise the date below.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information can be directed to the church office at or through our website.
